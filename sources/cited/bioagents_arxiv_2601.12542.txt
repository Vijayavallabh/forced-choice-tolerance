[abstract] Evaluation on the BixBench computational biology benchmark demonstrated state-of-the-art performance, achieving 48.8% accuracy on open response and 64.4% on multiple-choice evaluation, exceeding existing baselines by 14 to 26 percentage points.

[3.2.5 Benchmarking] The agent achieved state-of-the-art performance, with 48.8% accuracy on Open Response, 55.1% on MCQ with Refusal, and 64.4% on MCQ without Refusal.

[2.1] BixBench version v1.5 consists of 54 capsules containing real-world analytical scenarios spanning genomics, transcriptomics, differential expression analysis, RNA sequencing, phylogenetics and evolutionary analysis, whole genome sequencing, genomic variant analysis, and other bioinformatics categories (Mitchener et al., 2025a).
